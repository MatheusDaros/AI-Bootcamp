# AI Bootcamp

By [Encode Club](https://www.encode.club/) and [Venture Miner](https://www.ventureminer.com/)

## Introduction

This repository contains the instructions and resources for the AI Bootcamp. The bootcamp is a 6-week long program that aims to introduce students to the best techniques to creating Artificial Intelligence applications. The bootcamp is designed to be hands-on and project-based, where students will learn the concepts and apply them to real-world problems.

This bootcamp is **not** focused in the depths of AI and Machine Learning research, but rather in the **practical applications** of AI technology in many industries. The students will learn how to use the latest generative AI models and tools to build applications that are able to _understand_ and generate texts, images, and other types of content.

At the completion of the bootcamp, students will have an overview understanding of the latest AI models and tools, on AI techniques for image and text recognition and generation, on best practices and techniques, and will be able to build their own applications using AI features.

## Curriculum

The bootcamp content is divided in:

* Four weeks of hands-on lessons, each with classes from Monday to Thursday (four lessons per week) and a weekend project
* Onde special week with four days of guest lectures and workshops
* A final week of project building

## Bootcamp Material

The bootcamp material is available inside this repository. The material is organized in folders, with one lesson for each day of the bootcamp. Each folder contains the lesson material, containing the hands-on exercises and steps to follow during the lesson.

>Note: Each folder contains a `README.md` file with the instructions for the day. Students should clone the repository and prepare all of the required software and tools **before the lesson time**, so that they may follow the lessons and complete the exercises in parallel with the instructor.
>>Each lesson requires a handful of software and tools to be installed in the student's computer. The `README.md` file of each day contains the list of required software and tools, and the installation and configuration steps could take minutes, if not hours to complete, depending on the student's device.

## Hands-on Lessons

Students should prepare their own development environment to follow the lessons, and each day they will learn by repeating the steps of the instructor and applying the concepts at real-world projects.

>Node: The lessons will be recorded and available for later review at a slower pace, if needed.
>>The recorded material **must not** be shared with anyone outside the bootcamp.

Students will be able to ask questions and get help from the instructors and mentors at any time during the lessons, and also at the dedicated channels at the bootcamp's Discord server.

Students should work alongside peers on group projects, sharing their discoveries and learning from each other, and assisting each other for completing the given challenges of each project.

## Special Week of Guest Lectures and Workshops

Students will gain insights from guest experts and participate in exclusive workshops during the special week of the bootcamp. The guest lectures will cover a wide range of topics, from the latest research in AI to the practical applications of AI in the industry.

## Project Building Week

The last week is entirely dedicated to the students' projects. They will work in groups to build a project using the concepts learned during the bootcamp. Building a project is a non negotiable requirement for the bootcamp's completion and certification.
